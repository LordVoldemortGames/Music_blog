Concert: System Of A Down, Berlin 2026

System Of A Down live in Berlin

This concert was truly a one of a kind experience. The concert started with the first support act Acid Bath playing their song Tranquilized and concluded with System Of A Down's Sugar. From start to finish the concert had gone roughly four hours (including breaks), starting with Acid Bath playing a 30 minutes long set, which was an absolute highlight for me, as they count as one of the most influential bands in the sludge metal genre. After that the second support act, Queens of the Stone Age played a one hour long set, including Hits like The Lost Art of Keeping a secret, Go With the Flow and No One Known. Then, at around 8 p.m. SOAD opened with Soldier Side - Intro, playing a total of 31 songs over 1h 40min. The setlist included all of the big songs like B.Y.O.B., Chop Suey!, Lonely Day, Aerials and of course Toxicity. Though there was still some room left for some deeper cuts, especially from their self-titled debut including Darts or DAM from their 1995 Demo Tape 1.
