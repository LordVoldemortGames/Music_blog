My Favorite Albums Pt. 2: Toxicity by System of a Down

A little bit of history

Toxictiy is the second studio album by the American alt.- and nu-metal band System Of A Down, released on Septemper 4 2001. It was, like every other album of the band, by American producer Rick Rubin. While it's predecessor, the self-titled album, had a more raw less melodic sound, Toxicity managed to keep the same aggressive attitude while still featuring a melodic and diverse sound.

With the singles Chop Suey!, Toxicity and Aerials the album managed to reach top 50 chart positions in many countries and even Nr. 1 on the US Billboard Charts while selling over 8 million copies in total.

What I Like About This Album

System Of A Down, along with other bands like Linkin Park, got me into heavy music in the first place. However, Toxicity was the most influential album for me.

It's aggressive and heavy style instantly caught me while Serj Tankian's and Daron Malakian's lyrics about political and societal problems are still relevant to this day, even 25 years after it's original release. I'd even go as far as saying that today these lyrics are more important than ever with all the problems around the world. But not only the lyrics are timeless, but so is the albums sound. Daron's fast guitar riffs and Shavo Odadjian's and John Dolmayan's driving rythms force you to move while Serj's singing and shouting make you scream along. But even though most of the songs keep a quick pace, the album won't get boring even over the whole 14 (15 counting in the hidden track) songs, because this alternative metal sound mixed with other genres like trash, prog, jazz and even classic armenian music guarantees a variety over the whole album.
