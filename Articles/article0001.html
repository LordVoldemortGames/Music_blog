My Favorite Albums Pt. 1: Violator by Depeche Mode

A little bit of history

Violator is the seventh studio album by the british synth-pop band Depeche Mode, released in 1990. It is until today their most successful album with over 15 million copies sold.

The album features major hits like "Personal Jesus" and "Enjoy the Silence" which helped the album to reach the top ten of the charts in several countries. Further it is the first album of the band to feature acoustic instruments like guitars as can be heard in Personal Jesus.

What I Like About This Album

I think this album is a masterpiece in the synth-pop genre. The production quality is exceptional and it got some of the catchiest songs I know. The atmosphere of the album is for most parts dark and melancholic like in Halo or World in My Eyes, but it also got some more energetic songs like Personal Jesus or Enjoy the Silence. Dave Gahan's powerful and emotional voice is a perfect fit for this heavy sound while Martin Gore's high-pitched and almost even fragile vocals fit as a great contrast to Gahan's voice. It is also the album that got me into Depeche Mode and into synth-pop in general.
